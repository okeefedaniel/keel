- **`keel.geo`** — the suite's shared Connecticut town reference. `Municipality` (the 169 towns; `number` is the primary key and the only cross-product key, `slug` is immutable), `MunicipalityAlias` (villages and known misspellings, plus multi-town names that resolve to no town on purpose), `Municipality.resolve(text)` returning `(municipality, reason)`, and `Municipality.objects.resolver()` for bulk lookups at two queries total. `manage.py load_ct_municipalities` loads the bundled fixture idempotently; operator-added aliases (`source='admin'`) are never overwritten. Both models are registered in the Django admin. See "Geography (keel.geo)" in CLAUDE.md.
